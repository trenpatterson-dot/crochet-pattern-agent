# Product Backlog

This backlog captures product ideas from local research and manual feedback analysis. It is for planning only; backlog updates should not change runtime behavior by themselves.

## Market Research: Beginner Crochet Pattern Discovery

Research signal: beginner crocheters struggle to identify trusted pattern sources, prefer free or low-cost options, need written guidance plus tutorials, and want the app to explain why a recommendation is actually beginner-friendly.

### Near-Term

#### Trusted Source Labels

- Problem: Beginners do not always know whether a crochet site, blog, platform, or tutorial source is reliable.
- Backlog item: Add a visible trusted-source label in recommendation outputs when the source is known and appropriate.
- Product value: Helps beginners feel less like they are guessing between random links.
- Acceptance notes: Labels should be conservative and should not imply endorsement or quality guarantees without evidence.

#### Free/Low-Cost Preference

- Problem: Beginners often want to start with free or low-cost patterns before committing money.
- Backlog item: Make free or low-cost preference clearer in intake, filtering, and recommendation copy.
- Product value: Keeps recommendations aligned with beginner budget concerns.
- Acceptance notes: Do not claim a pattern is free or low-cost unless the source data supports it; avoid exact pricing unless verified.

#### Beginner Confidence Score

- Problem: "Beginner" labels are inconsistent, and users need to know why a pattern is safe to try.
- Backlog item: Add a beginner confidence score or label based on factors such as stitch complexity, project size, tutorial support, finishing clarity, and material difficulty.
- Product value: Explains why a pattern is beginner-friendly instead of just saying it is.
- Acceptance notes: Use cautious wording such as "beginner confidence" or "likely beginner-friendly"; avoid unsupported certainty.

### Later

#### Written + Video Guidance Preference

- Problem: Beginners may need both written instructions and tutorial support to follow through.
- Backlog item: Add a preference or ranking signal for patterns that include both written guidance and video/tutorial support.
- Product value: Matches the way many beginners actually learn: reading steps while watching the technique.
- Acceptance notes: Clearly distinguish official pattern instructions from external tutorial support.

#### Trusted Source Filter

- Problem: Source trust is not just a display concern; some users may want to filter out unknown or lower-confidence sources.
- Backlog item: Add a trusted-source filter after source labels have enough coverage to avoid hiding too many useful results.
- Product value: Gives beginners more control over recommendation safety and clarity.
- Acceptance notes: Build only after trusted-source labeling has been tested; avoid over-filtering when source evidence is incomplete.

### Needs More Evidence

- Which sources beginners already recognize as trustworthy.
- Whether users prefer a numeric beginner confidence score, a plain-language label, or both.
- Whether free/low-cost should be a hard filter or a ranking preference.
- Whether written-plus-video support matters more than project type for first-project completion.
- What evidence is enough to mark a source as trusted without making unsupported claims.
