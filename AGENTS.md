## Crochet Pattern Agent Positioning

Crochet Pattern Agent is for beginners who are tired of "easy" crochet patterns that are not actually easy.

The competition intelligence agent and any shared strategy prompts should evaluate competitors by how well they reduce beginner friction, not by pattern quantity or unsupported popularity claims.

Required beginner pain-point rubric:

1. Splitting Yarn
2. The Magic Ring
3. Losing Stitches
4. Tension Issues
5. Pattern Language
6. Hand Pain
7. Yarn Selection Confusion
8. Left-Handed Frustration
9. The Finishing Gap
10. Project Overwhelm

Competitor research should cover crochet websites, blogs, YouTube tutorials, Pinterest-style discovery, pattern platforms, beginner crochet tools, Etsy, and Ravelry. For each competitor, include the name/link, what they do well, pain points addressed, pain points missed, confusing beginner-experience areas, opportunities for Crochet Pattern Agent, recommended feature/content ideas, and an overall beginner-friendliness score from 1-10.
